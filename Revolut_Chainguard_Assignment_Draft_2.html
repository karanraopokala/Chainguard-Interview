What I learned from other vendors

I attended demos with these five providers to understand the differences between finding problems, fixing them and supplying maintained software.

Aikido

Scoping matters: dependency scanning, automated remediation and image hardening are different capabilities.

Sonatype

Safeguard

Two Buying Lenses

One account. Two different reasons to consider a change. These are hypotheses to test, not a claim that Revolut has agreed to a project.

CISO / VP Security

Hypothesis: reduce inherited software risk without disrupting existing controls.

Corporate objective

Support Revolut’s global banking growth with dependable services and demonstrable control over software risk.

Business objectives

  • Keep material software risk within the organisation’s tolerance while products and markets expand.
  • Give developers actionable security work without turning every finding into a manual escalation.
  • Produce the right component and change evidence for scoped regulatory and assurance reviews.
Why these objectives?+
Evidence
The account has published risk-based engineering controls and a developer-focused SCA investment. [01][08][18]
Why it matters
These are relevant leadership outcomes rather than a generic “more security” pitch.
What it does not prove
We have not confirmed the CISO’s current scorecard.
Ask in discovery
“Which software-security outcome is hardest to achieve with your current setup?”

Strategic initiatives

  • Operate the existing SCA/DAST programme and keep remediation useful for developers.
  • Maintain risk-based security checks as products and regulated banking operations expand.
  • Potential workstream: standardise approved image sources, component evidence and exception ownership with Platform Engineering.
Which are observed, and which are proposed?+
Evidence
Revolut has published its security tooling and release-control approach. [07][08]
Why it matters
Maintained container sources are a proposed extension to test, not a claimed active Revolut project.
What it does not prove
The public record does not show a Chainguard evaluation or a mandate to replace existing tools.
Ask in discovery

Risks & critical capabilities

  • Possible gap: inherited image findings still require investigation or exceptions. Need a breakdown by origin, not a total CVE count.
  • Change risk: introducing another source must not bypass existing scanning, evidence or release controls.
  • Priority risk: the remaining burden may be too small to justify another vendor.

Chainguard alignment

First call

Four questions, in order

  1. Who maintains and approves the base images today?
  2. When a new image CVE appears, what happens from finding to production fix?
  3. Which step takes meaningful time or creates exceptions?
  4. Is there a reason to change now, and who should validate it with us?

Follow the answer. Do not force all four questions into the opening.

Qualified next meeting

A narrow technical check

Invite the image owner and AppSec. Select one representative, non-critical image and agree what success means before a trial.

Compare: same application version, scanner, database date and severity scope; then test behaviour, migration effort and the work left for engineers.

Do not progress if there is no material gap, no owner, or no reason to act.

The decision to defend: Security is the best supported starting conversation, not a predetermined buyer. A useful first call can confirm the hypothesis, redirect it to Engineering, or disqualify the opportunity.

Terms used here

AppSec
Application Security: the function concerned with security in the applications a company builds.
SCA
Software Composition Analysis: identifies third-party components and associated vulnerability or licence issues.
DAST
Dynamic Application Security Testing: tests a running application from the outside.
Container image / base image
A packaged software environment; a base image is the starting image used to build another image.
CVE
An identifier for a disclosed vulnerability. A finding is not automatically an exploitable problem in a specific application.
SBOM / provenance
An inventory of software components / information about an artifact’s origin and build. Neither alone guarantees security.
Hypothesis
A reasoned possibility that discovery must validate. It is not a statement of an internal customer problem.
